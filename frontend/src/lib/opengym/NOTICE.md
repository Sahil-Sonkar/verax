Exercise names, instructions, stills and GIFs come from
[hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset](https://github.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset)
and keep that dataset's own terms. Media is loaded from jsDelivr at runtime, not bundled.

Guided-workout helpers (1RM, rest timer, wake lock, catalog lookup) are adapted from
[openGym](https://github.com/arvids-unavailable/openGym) (GNU AGPL v3.0).
